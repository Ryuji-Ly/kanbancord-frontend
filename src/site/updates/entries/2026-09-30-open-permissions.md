---
title: Open permissions and a board's own feed settings
date: 2026-09-30
---
- **Open permissions:** for small groups that trust each other, everyone who can talk in the server can create, change and move everything on its boards. Switch it on with `/kanbancord features` or in the server's features.
- **A board's own feed settings:** change which events a feed posts about one board, without changing the feed for the others.
- Comments can only be edited by the person who wrote them.
- Every view in Discord leads onward, and every action is offered to everyone who can see it; whether they may do it is checked when they click.
- A tip for managers of a brand-new server on how simple mode works.
