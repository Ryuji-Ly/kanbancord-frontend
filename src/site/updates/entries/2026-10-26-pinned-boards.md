---
title: Pinned boards and a board menu
date: 2026-10-26
---
- **Pin boards to the dashboard:** pinned boards from every server you are in appear at the top of the dashboard, above the server you have open, so the boards you use most are one click away. Pins are saved with your account and follow you to every device.
- **Each board has a ⋯ menu** on its card and on the board page: pin or unpin it, open its settings, export it, archive or restore it, or delete it. The settings are now only about configuring the board.
