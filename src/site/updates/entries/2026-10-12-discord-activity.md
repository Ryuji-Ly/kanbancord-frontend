---
title: KanbanCord as a Discord Activity
date: 2026-10-12
---
- **Open your boards inside Discord:** start the KanbanCord Activity from a voice channel's Activities menu, or from the app launcher in any text channel. Everyone in the call sees the same board, live, and Discord signs you in.
- Changes made in the Activity show as such in the audit log.
- **Colour-blind friendly themes** now keep every status colour clearly apart.
