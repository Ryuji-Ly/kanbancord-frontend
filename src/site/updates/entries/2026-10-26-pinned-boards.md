---
title: Pinned boards, a board menu, and importing tasks
date: 2026-10-26
---
- **Pin boards to the dashboard:** pinned boards from every server you are in appear at the top of the dashboard, above the server you have open, so the boards you use most are one click away. Pins are saved with your account and follow you to every device.
- **Each board has a ⋯ menu** on its card and on the board page: pin or unpin it, open its settings, export it, archive or restore it, or delete it. The settings are now only about configuring the board.
- **Import tasks into a board** from its ⋯ menu on the website, or with `/task import` in Discord. Paste a list, one task per line, with or without bullets, numbers or checkboxes: indented items become a checklist in the task, headings start a column, and ticked items go to the last column. Spreadsheet cells, CSV and Markdown tables and KanbanCord board files work too, with their columns, due dates, priorities and labels matched to the board.
- **A preview comes first**, showing which column each task goes to and anything left out, such as labels the board does not have. Nothing changes until you import, and the whole import is one change in the audit log and feeds.
