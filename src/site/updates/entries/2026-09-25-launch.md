---
title: KanbanCord launches
date: 2026-09-25
---
The bot and the website go live.

- **Boards from Discord:** create boards, columns and tasks, move and assign them, set due dates and comment with `/board`, `/column`, `/task` and `/comment`.
- **The website:** whole boards at a glance, with drag and drop, search and filters, and images and videos in task descriptions.
- **Permissions that follow your Discord roles** from the start, fine-tuned per board and per person on the website.
- **Simple mode:** labels, priorities, assignees, comments, due dates and custom permissions stay off until a server switches them on. Each board can switch them off again for itself.
- **Assign roles** as well as people.
- **Notifications:** update feeds in the channels you choose, an audit log channel that never pings, direct messages about your own tasks, and reminders before tasks are due.
- **Themes and accessibility options**, including high contrast and a larger text size.
- `/help` lists every command, and `/report` sends a bug report or idea straight to the developer.
