---
title: Import and export, /audit, and who else is in the Activity
date: 2026-10-18
---
- **Export a board** as a file, from its settings on the website or with `/board export`. Its columns and tasks are always included; labels, priorities, assignees, comments and permission rules are up to you.
- **Import a board** from such a file as a new board, here or in another server: use the arrow next to Create board, or `/board import`. People and roles that are not in the server are left out, and you are told how many.
- `/audit` **browses the audit log in Discord**, filtered by board, person or kind of change.
- **See who else is in the Activity:** the faces of everyone else in the same Activity session appear at the top.
- **A guide to the Discord Activity**, on the website.
- **Posting a board again in a channel replaces the older post there:** the old one links to the new one and stops updating.
- Moving tasks, permission checks and the website's first load are faster.
