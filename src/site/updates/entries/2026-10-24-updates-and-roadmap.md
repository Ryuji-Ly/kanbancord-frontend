---
title: Updates, a public roadmap, and archiving tasks
date: 2026-10-24
---
- **This page:** what has changed in KanbanCord, newest first. It is linked at the top of every page.
- **The roadmap:** KanbanCord's own board, open to everyone, showing what is being worked on, what is planned and what is done.
- **Report an issue on the roadmap:** press Report beside its title and sign in with Discord to add a bug or an idea to its Issues column, one an hour.
- **Very long columns fold:** a column taller than two screens is cut off there, with a button at the bottom to show the rest.
- Updates and the roadmap are linked from the dashboard too. On phones, the site's links are behind a menu button, so the header stays readable.
- **Archive a task** to take it off the board without deleting it: from the task on the website, from its menu in Discord, or with `/task archive`. It keeps its details, people, labels and comments, but cannot be changed until it is restored.
- **Archive every task in a column at once**, such as a long Done column: from the column's ⋯ menu on the website, the column's view in Discord, or `/column archive-tasks`. Feeds and the audit log show it as one change, not one per task.
- **Find and restore archived tasks:** on the website, choose Archived tasks in the board's filters and open one; in Discord, use `/task restore`. A restored task goes back to the end of its column.
- `/help` now shows its commands a few at a time, with Previous and Next.
