---
title: The website in eight more languages
date: 2026-10-09
---
- **The website is now in French, German, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Slovak, Spanish and Turkish.** Pick a language in Preferences, or request one that is missing from the language list.
- **Preferences and settings in one dialog**, with every section on one page.
- **When something stops working, KanbanCord says what:** if the bot loses access to a feed, audit log or board post channel, it names the missing permission and the channel, and tells the server.
- New labels and priority levels can be made right from a task's panels in Discord.
- The bot's status shows live counts of servers, members, boards and tasks.
- The About page tells how KanbanCord came to be.
