---
title: Board posts, interactive feeds and guides
date: 2026-09-28
---
- **Board posts:** `/board post` puts a whole board in a channel or thread as one message that updates itself whenever the board changes. Anyone can open its columns and tasks from the post.
- **Interactive feeds:** a feed can post the whole task with buttons to move, assign or edit it right there.
- **Follow a task** to hear about its changes, from Discord or the website.
- **Mentions per event:** choose for each kind of change whether a feed mentions the people it concerns.
- `/label` and `/priority` manage a board's labels and priority levels from Discord.
- `/guide` walks you through setting up a server step by step, and `/kanbancord features` switches simple mode's features on and off.
- The new-task form only asks for what your server uses.
- **Guides** on the website, with screenshots, for every part of KanbanCord.
