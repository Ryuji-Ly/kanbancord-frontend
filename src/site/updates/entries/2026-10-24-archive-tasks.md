---
title: Archive tasks
date: 2026-10-24
---
- **Archive a task** to take it off the board without deleting it: from the task on the website, from its menu in Discord, or with `/task archive`. It keeps its details, people, labels and comments, but cannot be changed until it is restored.
- **Archive every task in a column at once**, such as a long Done column: from the column's ⋯ menu on the website, the column's view in Discord, or `/column archive-tasks`. Feeds and the audit log show it as one change, not one per task.
- **Find and restore archived tasks:** on the website, choose Archived tasks in the board's filters and open one; in Discord, use `/task restore`. A restored task goes back to the end of its column.
