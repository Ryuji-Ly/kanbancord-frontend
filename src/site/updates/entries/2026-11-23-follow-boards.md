---
title: Follow a whole board
date: 2026-11-23
---
- **Follow a board** to hear about every task on it by direct message, new ones included, as if you followed each one: from the board's ⋯ menu on the website, with `/board follow`, or with the button on a board's view in Discord. Only you are told.
- **New tasks on one board only:** follow that board and switch on Task created in your notification settings. Until now, Task created almost never sent anything, because you only hear about tasks you are connected to, and nobody is connected to a task before it exists.
- What you hear about is still up to your own settings, and "tasks I follow" covers the boards you follow too.
