---
title: Scrolling wide boards with a mouse
date: 2026-11-07
---
- **Boards wider than the screen have a scrollbar again**, and it stays at the bottom of the window however long the columns are.
- **Drag the empty space between columns** to move the board sideways.
- **Dragging a task or column to the left or right edge** scrolls the board, so it can go to a column that is off screen.
- Scrollbars across the website now use the theme's colours.
