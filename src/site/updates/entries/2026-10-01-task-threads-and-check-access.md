---
title: Task threads, Create task and Check access
date: 2026-10-01
---
- **A thread per task:** `/board threads` gives each task its own thread in one of the board's feed channels, public or private. Threads follow the task's title and close when it is deleted.
- **Discuss in thread:** a button on tasks that opens the task's thread, or makes it there and then.
- **Create task from a message:** right-click or long-press a message, then Apps → Create task. The task links back to the message.
- **Check access:** see what anyone may do on a server or board, and which rule decided it.
- Feed events, mentions and boards, a board's own feed settings, and the events you are messaged about can all be changed from Discord now.
- Stop a server's direct messages right from one of them.
- The bot refuses a feed or audit log channel it could not post in, and says why.
- Every `/guide` step is ticked off as your server completes it.
- A full guide to permissions, with every case explained.
