---
title: Only people who use KanbanCord are stored
date: 2026-10-31
---
- **KanbanCord no longer keeps a copy of every server member.** It keeps the people who use it on a server, and the ones someone assigns or names in a permission rule. Everyone else is looked up in Discord when needed: searching for someone to assign, or for a permission rule, still finds anyone in the server.
- People who are in no server with KanbanCord any more, and made nothing that still shows their name, are removed within two days. The [privacy policy](/privacy) says what is kept and for how long.
