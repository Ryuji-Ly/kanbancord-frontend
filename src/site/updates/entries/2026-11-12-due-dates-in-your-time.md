---
title: Due dates in your own time, and replies to /report
date: 2026-11-12
---
- **Due dates picked on the website are now in your own time zone**, as they look. Before, they were saved as UTC, so outside UTC reminders, the overdue filter and Discord could be hours off from what the website showed. Due dates set earlier on the website may be off by your difference from UTC: check the ones that matter.
- **Replies to `/report`:** the developer can now answer an issue or suggestion you sent, by direct message from KanbanCord.
- **Times you type in Discord are in your own time zone too.** Discord does not tell bots anyone's time zone, so the website saves yours from your device when you open it. If you only use Discord, choose it with `/notifications timezone:`, such as `Europe/Berlin` or `UTC+8`. Until one is known, times are read as UTC and the reply says so; typing an offset, like `2026-11-20 17:00 +08:00`, still works.
